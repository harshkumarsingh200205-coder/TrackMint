TrackMint Dashboard Guide

Overview: TrackMint provides high-velocity personal expense tracking and monthly budget insights.

  • Switch Currency: Use the top-left currency trigger to view amounts in USD, EUR, GBP, or INR.
  • Add Expenses: Click "Transfer" or use the top action button to log an expense.
  • Set Budget: Click "Request" to update your monthly spending ceiling.
  • Table Controls: Search, filter by status, select rows, and export records as CSV.
  • Light/Dark Mode: Click the sun/moon icon at the top of the left dock.
